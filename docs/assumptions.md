# Product assumptions

- All included titles and artwork are fictional and original demo material.
- ChatGPT sign-in is the identity provider for the Sites deployment.
- D1 and R2 replace PostgreSQL and S3 for the deployed Sites edition.
- Payments and memberships remain in test or disabled state until provider
  secrets, tax configuration, and webhook verification are installed.
- Team payouts remain disabled until legal, tax, identity, and processor
  requirements are complete.
- The reader's geometric chapter pages are demo content, not copyrighted comic
  pages.
- Public direct messages are excluded from the initial release.
- Mature content is disabled by default.
